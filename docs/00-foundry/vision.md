# Foundry Vision

**Foundation:** 1.0-rc1  
**Authority:** Product vision (supporting context; principles and mission outrank this file)

## Product hierarchy

```text
Foundry Platform
└── Agent City
    └── V1 Operational Neighborhood
```

Foundry is a spatial operating platform for autonomous organizations. It enables humans to supervise, govern, train, and evolve real autonomous work through persistent virtual environments where meaningful visual elements correspond to actual operational entities, capabilities, states, transfers, and events.

Agent City is the first spatial application on Foundry. Agent City V1 is the first operational neighborhood inside that application.

Foundry must not be reduced to a game, decorative virtual world, conventional dashboard, AI chat interface, or Agent City alone. Do not describe Foundry as an operating system unless a future approved ADR adopts that classification.

## The problem Foundry solves

Autonomous work spreads across terminals, tickets, logs, chats, and dashboards. Operators lose situational awareness: who is working, what is blocked, what failed, what needs approval, and what completed.

Foundry makes autonomous organizations inspectable by binding operational truth to a persistent spatial world and complementary precision interface—so governance is continuous, not reconstructed after the fact.

## Why spatial interfaces help

Space communicates relationship:

- Residences hold identity.
- Workplaces hold execution.
- Institutions hold governance and policy.
- Roads and vehicles show permitted movement and transfers.
- Construction shows incomplete capability.
- Upgrades show verified maturity.

A well-designed operational world lets an operator grasp health, ownership, bottlenecks, and progression in seconds, then drill into exact evidence in Command Mode.

## Representation versus simulation

| Representing real work | Simulating fictional work |
| --- | --- |
| Visual state maps to backend entities and events | Visuals invent progress for atmosphere |
| Transfers require recorded prerequisites | Packages “move” because an animation finished |
| Failures stay inspectable | Failures become vague mood |
| Ambient motion is labeled as non-operational | Ambient motion implies productivity |

Meaningful animations require declared operational events. Ambient animation may create atmosphere but cannot imply false activity.

## Long-term applicability

Foundry’s spatial operating model is intended to generalize beyond software builds:

- companies and multi-tenant campuses
- infrastructure and facilities operations
- manufacturing and production chains
- robotics fleets
- smart properties and building systems
- other autonomous organizations where humans must govern machine work

V1 proves the model on one software-build neighborhood. Broader domains remain Future Registry or later missions.

## Human governance

Agents execute within explicit authority. Humans govern.

Approvals, pauses, revisions, and upgrades leave auditable records. High-risk actions must not hide behind animations or conversational summaries. The 3D world communicates broad state; the 2D interface provides exact data, evidence, controls, logs, and approvals.

## What V1 must prove

That one operator, on an ultrawide command-center display, can supervise one AI-assisted software-build workflow end-to-end—including intentional failure, blocked transfer, independent validation, human approval, capability-based upgrade, and persistence across reload—more clearly than disconnected terminals and dashboards.
