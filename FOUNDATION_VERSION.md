## Authority

Active governing documents under `docs/00-foundry/`, `docs/01-mission/`, `docs/02-specification/`, and `docs/03-architecture/` constitute **Foundry Foundation 1.0-rc1**.

This baseline is **not** final or approved. It must not be treated as implementation-ready until the foundation audit is written, reviewed, and blocking findings are resolved.

`docs/archive/foundation-v0/` is historical only and must not be used for implementation.

## Change control

- Clarifications that do not change meaning: update documents and `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Changes to principles, mission scope, domain language, or ADRs: update this file and `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Promotion from `1.0-rc1` to `1.0` requires reviewed audit and explicit approval.
